What is High Value Loot?

High Value Loot (HVL) is the currency of Vulture's reputation. Every item you sell him builds your rank, unlocking better gear, safes, and access to weapons you can't get anywhere else. He pays less than the market rate — but the rep is worth it.

Prices marked with ✦ are confirmed from in-game sales. All others are based on current community data and will be updated as 0.4 Spearhead prices stabilize.
