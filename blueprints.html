Blueprints

Lamang gives up its secrets slowly. Scattered across the island — in places most operators never think to look — are magazines. Not just ammunition. Proof.

Each one is tied to a weapon. Find it, and word gets back to the right people. Complete the job they ask of you, and that variant becomes yours to purchase. No questions asked.

Nine magazines. Nine weapons. Vulture knows where they came from. He's not saying.

Range & Rifle Magazines

Range & Rifle Magazines don't show up on any manifest. They surface in the field, in places that see a lot of violence. Each one unlocks something — a camo variant, a weapon that looks like it survived something it probably shouldn't have.

Find the magazine. Do the job. The weapon is yours. There are nine of them out there. Most operators find one by accident. The smart ones go looking.
