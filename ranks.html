VULTURE RANKS

HOW TO RANK UP

To increase your Vulture rank in Gray Zone Warfare, you must sell him HVL—High Value Loot. This includes rare and valuable items like watches, jewelry, cameras, electronics, credit cards, and alcohol. The more valuable the item, the more reputation you earn.

⚠️ Note: Vulture only buys your HVL for half of what the highest offer is from the other vendors for your items. You do not gain rep by buying items from Vulture—only selling HVL increases your rank. For example, Majestic Titanium Watch normally sells for 6K, but from Vulture, it sells for 3K.

Ranking up Vulture unlocks exclusive weapons, gear, and high-tier rewards. Keep looting, focus on valuable items, and sell directly to Vulture to level up fast.

RANK REWARDS

  • Rank 1: Basic 1911 Pistol Parts

    Start small with essentials for your secondary weapon.

  • Rank 2: MP7A2 Submachine Gun

    A compact, high fire-rate SMG for close-quarters combat.

  • Rank 3: Custom SVD Marksman Rifle

    Reliable DMR perfect for long-range engagements.

  • Rank 4: AK-308 Battle Rifle

    Heavy-hitting 7.62 rifle for high damage and precision.

PRO TIPS TO LEVEL UP

  • Prioritize HVL items and horde them in your stash till you can visit Vulture.
  • Learn where the high-value loot spawns.
  • Don't get greedy—extract early if you’re holding big value.
