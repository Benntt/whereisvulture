    Who is Vulture?

    Vulture is a mythic black market vendor in Gray Zone Warfare. Scavenger, trader, and survivor. Each week, he relocates to two Major CoPs across Lamang, appearing every Monday at 3:00 PM UTC (local time).

    He collects gear from fallen players, restores it to 95% durability, and sells it back, often at a price. Players can also trade HVL (High Value Loot) to increase their rank with him and unlock better gear.

    Ex-military? Local rogue? No one knows for sure. But if you find him, your squad gets paid.
