In Gray Zone Warfare, Vulture is a shadowy black market vendor operating deep in Lamang — a man of myth, whispers, and war stories. No one knows his real name or where he came from, but those lucky enough to find him know this: he's got items you want.

Vulture appears at only two Major Combat Outposts (CoPs) each week, chosen from a rotating pool across all servers. He resets every Monday at 3:00 PM UTC ( local time), and once he arrives, it's a race to find him before the week ends.

According to official lore, Vulture was active in the underground weapons trade long before the Lamang Island evacuation. When chaos took over, he saw opportunity — growing his black market empire in the shadows of military collapse.

He deals in high-tier, scavenged loot: weapons and gear dropped by fallen players, salvaged from the battlefield and restored to at least 95% durability before resale. If the item is below 95% when found, Vulture repairs it himself. He’s a true scavenger.

Some items are dynamic, changing based on what’s recovered — but a few static goods are always stocked. Players can also trade HVL (High Value Loot) to increase their rank with Vulture, unlocking better items and exclusive gear.

"HE DOESN’T CARE WHO YOU ARE — ONLY WHAT YOU CAN TRADE."

Some say he’s ex-military. Others say he's a local gone rogue. Whatever the truth, finding Vulture means payday for your squad. Bookmark this site to track his real-time location and stay up to date. If you want to find Vulture, you’ll need timing, luck, and maybe a little help.
